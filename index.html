05 / METHODOLOGY

From field readings to a digital map

Mixed-method, participatory action research combining quantitative measurements with community observations and interviews.

01

Measure

PM2.5 and PM10 were recorded using a portable low-cost particulate sensor; noise was recorded using a smartphone sound-level application checked against a reference meter.

02

Locate

GPS coordinates were noted for each sampling site, allowing measurements to be presented spatially.

03

Observe

Traffic density, honking, idling vehicles, construction, waste burning and other visible pollution sources were documented.

04

Analyse

Readings were entered into a spreadsheet, cleaned, averaged and compared with NAAQS, WHO guidelines and relevant noise limits.

05

Visualise

The report used Python, pandas, folium and matplotlib concepts to produce maps and charts that non-experts can understand.

06

Engage

Maps, posters, bilingual pamphlets, video and interactive quizzes translated data into community awareness and action.

07 / RECOMMENDATIONS

Turning findings into local action

Seven recommendations presented in the project report.

01

Community monitoring network

Train students, residents and volunteers to take regular readings at fixed locations.

02

Low-cost sensors + open dashboard

Deploy calibrated sensors at key junctions, schools and hospitals and publish live readings.

03

Silence-zone enforcement

Strengthen signage, enforcement and “no honking” campaigns near sensitive locations.

04

Control dust & waste burning

Promote construction dust controls and discourage open burning of waste.

05

Simplify grievance channels

Make pollution complaint mechanisms easier to find, understand and use.

06

Annual awareness events

Institutionalise awareness activities with the college and local groups.

07

Policy advocacy & research

Share findings with authorities and repeat the study with longer periods and more sites.

PROJECT TAKEAWAY

Maps make invisible problems visible.

Technology becomes meaningful when it helps a community understand its surroundings and act on local evidence.
